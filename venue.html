The Clearwater Ski Hill

Clearwheezin’ takes place at the Clearwater Ski Hill, a community run venue just above Clearwater, BC. The base area serves as race headquarters, start and finish, aid station, social hub, camping area, and the place where the gong gets rung.

From there, the course climbs directly into forested trail terrain, delivering real elevation, real repetition, and no shortcuts.

Getting Here

566 Dunn Lake Rd, Clearwater BC V0E 1N0

By Car

Clearwater is located along Highway 5 in the North Thompson Valley, approximately 1.5 hours north of Kamloops and 4.5 hours northeast of Vancouver. Follow signs for Clearwater and the Clearwater Ski Hill once you are in town.

Open map

In Town

The Clearwater Ski Hill is a short drive from downtown Clearwater across the North Thompson River. Parking is available at the base area.
